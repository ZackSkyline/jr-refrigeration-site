JR Refrigeration installs and services air conditioning and heating for homes and businesses across the Rio Grande Valley.

What we believe

A new air conditioning system is one of the biggest purchases most families make for their home. You should get a clear price before the work starts, a complete system installed the right way, and a written warranty you can keep. That is the whole business.

What we do

  • New central A/C systems, 3 to 5 tons, with the indoor unit with heating and the outdoor unit
  • Full system replacements
  • Ductless mini splits, single and multi-zone
  • Heating installation and service
  • Commercial air conditioning and heating
